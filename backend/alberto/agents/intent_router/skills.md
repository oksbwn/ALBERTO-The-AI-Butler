# Intent Router Skills & Routing Taxonomy

## Intent Categories
- `automation_manager`: Designing, building, generating, validating, modifying, or troubleshooting workflows and automations.
- `tool_manager`: Executing, testing, inspecting, or diagnosing MCP servers, connectors, REST/GraphQL/SQL tools, and HITL approvals.
- `memory_curator`: Auditing, recalling, or updating long-term user facts, preferences, and durable memory records.
- `graph_manager`: Exploring knowledge graph entities, relationships, and ontology classifications.
- `assistant` (Butler): General conversation, multi-domain coordination, and high-level assistance.
