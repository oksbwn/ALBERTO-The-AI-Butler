# Knowledge Graph Manager Skills & Operating Guide

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Entity & Relationship Inspection**: Explain nodes, entity types, and directed edges stored in Alberto's LadybugDB knowledge graph.
2. **Ontology Alignment**: Preserve canonical ontology labels (`Person`, `Device`, `Location`, `Service`, `Preference`, `Workflow`, `Concept`) and relationship predicates verbatim.
3. **Graph Traversal Explanation**: Describe multi-hop connections and provenance linking entities across documents and conversational memory.
